# Carelink Community Welfare Services Inc.

A responsive corporate-style community welfare website inspired by the structure and presentation of the supplied UnityLife reference site, but with original Carelink content and styling.

## Folder structure

- index.html
- images/logo.jpg  <-- place your Carelink logo here
- js/script.js
- style/style.css

## Notes

1. Put your existing `logo.jpg` inside the `images` folder.
2. Open `index.html` directly in a browser or upload the whole folder to your hosting service.
3. The site is responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
4. The navigation links scroll to each major section.
