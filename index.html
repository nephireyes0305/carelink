ABOUT CARELINK

Care that begins with community.

Carelink Community Welfare Services Inc. is a community-driven organization dedicated to providing timely financial assistance, compassionate support, and meaningful protection to individuals and families during life's most challenging moments.

We believe that no family should face an emergency alone. Our organization was established with a simple yet powerful purpose—to unite communities through a sustainable welfare program that provides assistance during accidents, hospitalization, disability, bereavement, and other unforeseen hardships.

At Carelink, every member becomes part of a caring network committed to helping one another. Through affordable membership, transparent governance, and community partnerships, we strive to make emergency assistance accessible to every family while promoting preparedness, compassion, and social responsibility.

OUR PURPOSE

Turning shared responsibility into real support.

Carelink Community Welfare Services Inc. was founded to create a sustainable community where members voluntarily support one another through collective participation and shared responsibility.

Our purpose is to provide timely assistance during life's unexpected emergencies, including hospitalization, accidental death, natural death, disaster response, livelihood support, educational initiatives, and other community welfare programs.

Through unity, transparency, and responsible stewardship, we empower ordinary individuals to become extraordinary instruments of hope for one another.
